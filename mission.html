Who We Are

DNA Girls Flag Football was created with one goal in mind: to develop the youth and provide girls with a pathway to reach their fullest potential.

We are starting with the younger generations of athletes and building something that grows with them. Our vision is not simply to create teams for one season or one age group. We are building a long-term player development pathway that begins with our youngest athletes and develops with them as they grow, mature, and advance through the sport.

As girls flag football continues to grow at an unprecedented rate and becomes an increasingly recognized pathway to high school and collegiate athletics, we believe girls deserve more than the traditional model of "sign up, practice, and play for your town."

Instead of jumping between different programs, coaches, and teams as they get older, DNA athletes will have access to a structured development system designed to grow with them.

A young athlete entering DNA today may begin by learning the fundamentals, building confidence, and developing a love for the game. As she progresses, she will have the opportunity to advance her skills, learn more advanced concepts, compete at higher levels, and eventually become part of our elite teams and exposure opportunities.

The goal is to develop our own athletes from the ground up.
Our youngest players today are the high school and collegiate athletes of tomorrow.

Athletes will have the opportunity to remain within the DNA system as they grow and develop, building on the skills and knowledge they have already acquired rather than starting over every time they move to a new team or program.

Our younger athletes will have access to the same DNA philosophy, resources, technology, testing, and development mindset that our older athletes receive. As they mature, their opportunities will grow with them. The ultimate goal is to have athletes who have been developed within the DNA system from a young age through the highest levels of competition they are capable of reaching.

What Makes DNA Different

We are committed to offering opportunities and resources that no other program in our area currently provides. DNA will bring together:

  • Elite, experienced coaching
  • Advanced training and player-development methods
  • Elite-level teams and competition
  • Year-round development and consistent training
  • Combine testing and measurable athletic development
  • Game and combine film opportunities
  • A direct pathway toward high school and collegiate exposure
  • Individual digital athlete profiles through the DNA App

Our younger athletes will begin building their DNA profile and athletic history from the start. As they grow, their profile grows with them.

Our Mission

To develop young female athletes through elite coaching, advanced training, consistent competition, and meaningful opportunities for growth and exposure while helping each athlete reach her fullest potential on and off the field.

We aren't simply trying to build better football players. We are developing confident, disciplined, competitive young women who understand what it means to work toward a goal, overcome challenges, be part of a team, and pursue excellence.

Our mission begins with our youngest athletes. We want them to develop the right way from the beginning, establish strong fundamentals, learn how to compete, and grow within a culture that challenges them to become better every day.

Our Vision

To become the premier girls flag football development program in New Jersey and create a true pathway from youth flag football to high school and collegiate opportunities.

We envision a program where girls have one place to call home—a place where they can train, compete, develop, build relationships, track their progress, and pursue their goals alongside the best coaches and athletes in the area.

A place where an 8-year-old athlete can enter DNA, develop year after year, advance through the program, compete on elite teams, build her athletic profile, gain exposure, and ultimately pursue opportunities at the high school and collegiate levels.

As the sport continues to grow and collegiate recruitment opportunities rapidly expand, DNA intends to be ahead of the curve—not catching up to it.
