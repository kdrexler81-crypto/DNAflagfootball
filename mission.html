Our Mission

Elevating Girls' Flag Football through elite development, professional coaching, and national exposure.

The DNA Difference

We don't just run plays; we build athletes. Our goal is to provide a premier, long-term pathway for girls to reach their fullest potential on and off the field.

  • Elite Pipeline: Escaping the limits of recreational leagues to compete at a travel and national level.
  • High-Volume Fundamentals: Mastering the mechanics of the game from day one.
  • Next-Level Exposure: Dual-sanctioned by USA Football and NFL FLAG.
